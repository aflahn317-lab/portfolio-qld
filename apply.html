Who is a Client Representative?

A Client Representative is an independent partner who identifies businesses that need digital transformation—whether it's a new website, a custom SaaS platform, or AI integration.

You act as the bridge. You find the lead, set up the initial meeting, and understand their high-level requirements. We handle the technical consultation, closing the deal, and writing the code. When they pay, you get paid.
